Staff

The team that runs UDA, hosts the events, and keeps competition fair.

Roles

Leadership

Bruhman, the founder, leads the United Dueling Amity. He sets the direction for UDA and oversees every part of the organization.

UDA Leads

Second in command, with nearly the same authority as the founder. UDA Leads govern the Moderators and oversee the rest of the server.

Moderators

UDA's core staff team. They keep the server safe, respectful, and focused on competition.

UDA Artists

Create the art and graphics that represent the United Dueling Amity.

The UDSC

The United Dueling Supreme Court oversees clan servers and rules on disputes. Clan leaders may be summoned to present their case.
